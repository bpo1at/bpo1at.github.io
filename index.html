Classic Mini Games Offline

Four classic games in one app, all playable without an internet connection: Minesweeper, Sudoku, Yatzy (solo, against a bot, or with friends on the same phone) and Battleship (against the computer or a friend on the same phone).

  • Your progress and statistics stay on your device.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Pass-and-play modes for two or more people, no online connection needed.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All games work offline. Ads, when shown, need a connection; without one the games simply continue without ads.

Where is my progress stored?

Saved games, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

How do I play with friends?

Games that offer a friends mode have a "Play vs friends" (or "Play vs a friend") option on their start page. Choose it, then pass the phone around when the screen asks you to. In games with hidden information, such as Battleship, a cover screen hides the boards when the phone changes hands.

How can I limit ad personalization?

On Android: Settings → Google → Ads (delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization). In the app you can also review your consent choice where it is offered.
